# When You're In Every Store, Control Matters

**Target audience:** Gas Gang, Drizzle Factory, and large Indigenous market distributors
**Length:** ~2:30–3:00
**Style:** Real talk with receipts — not a corporate pitch deck
**Tone:** Conversational, confident, a bit cheeky. Like a homie showing you something obvious you've been missing.
**Pacing:** Punchy. No long pauses. Keep it moving.

> Every flavour listed. Every image correct.
>
> No more "Random Flavour."
> No more "Hybrid."
> Every customer sees exactly what you sell.

**Visuals:** Traffic POS admin — product creation screen showing a vape with all flavours defined. Then storefront showing the same product with full flavour dropdown. Before/after split: the sad Canafast listing vs. a clean Traffic Store listing with every flavour.

**Visual source:** Scout Traffic POS admin + storefront (real screenshots of your system)
